Changes and checks
------------------
- Keep the fixnum slot scan, cache the structure-class layout-slot location
  during cold generation, and read that Lisp-value slot directly.
- Inline small standard constructors while retaining named calls for unknown
  or computed keyword lists. Saved constructors retain redefinition handling.
- Resolve the class in the compiler before inlining allocation. An initial
  attempt to inline %ALLOCATE-STRUCT directly bypassed this rewrite and
  reintroduced FIND-CLASS on every allocation (~29 s); it was rejected.
- Inline the known-class allocation helper and %ALLOCATE-INSTANCE, preserving
  the general checked entry point. The helper reads the current layout, so
  a compiled allocator still follows subsequent structure redefinition.
- Preserve imported uninterned literal identity across compiled files. Tokens
  from existing constructor definitions cannot be copied into another LLF;
  such imported expansions retain named calls. Current-file definitions still
  inline. Parallel compiler workers share the same per-file definition set.

The latest helper change alone measured 3.57 -> 3.00 s median in its controlled
comparison; that is incremental to prior allocator and constructor changes.

Fresh cold build, cold startup (48.39 s, 2.44 s GC), snapshot, and reboot passed.
Fresh-image tests passed: allocation cache (including a precompiled allocator
used after redefinition and invalid inputs at high safety), compatible layouts,
reparenting, constructor inlining, and imported/same-file literal identity in
both serial and forced-parallel compilation. The layout suite also passed two
successive compile/load/run cycles after the literal-identity fix.
No structure layout changed; no make clean was used.

Profiler/GDB analysis for next session
--------------------------------------
Final direct samples (272 total): MAKE-TREE 34.9%, POPULATE 34.6%, low-level bump
allocator 21.0%, allocation wrapper 3.3%. Inlined allocation instructions are
attributed to MAKE-TREE/POPULATE, so these percentages do not imply all of that
time is tree traversal. GDB confirms direct layout/size/area reads followed by
%ALLOCATE-OBJECT, without FIND-CLASS or the former allocation helper chain.
It also shows a generic integer shift/conversion for LOCATION-OFFSET-T remains
on the hot path, including %%MAKE-SIGNED-BYTE-64-RAX. Caching the word offset
instead of repeatedly decoding the encoded location is a candidate to measure.
Other candidates: allocator metering/wrapper cost and recursive-call overhead.
No further optimization was attempted after the user's session-wrap request.

GC is ~13-14% of final elapsed time. Eliminating it entirely would give only
~1.15x further speedup, so most of the remaining SBCL gap is outside GC.
